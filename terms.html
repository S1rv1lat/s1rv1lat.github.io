Use information

Effective 31 October 2026

Personal tool

YouTube Channel Analytics is operated by the owner of Small Tales, Big History for their own channel. It is not offered as a public or paid service and does not offer account registration on this website.

Read-only analysis

The collector retrieves information authorised by the channel owner and creates local reports. Recommendations are suggestions for the owner's review. They do not guarantee views, revenue, subscriber growth or any other outcome. API data can be delayed, incomplete, revised or unavailable.

Owner control

The channel owner controls Google authorisation, local collection and stored reports, and decides whether to act on recommendations. The collector has no permission to upload or change videos. Access can be revoked through Google Account connections.
