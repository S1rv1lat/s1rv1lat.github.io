YouTube Channel Analytics

A personal, read-only tool for understanding the performance of an illustrated history channel.

What the tool does

The channel owner uses this local desktop tool to retrieve video information and YouTube Analytics reports, including views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and available thumbnail reach statistics. Local reports help the owner evaluate content and plan improvements.

This website provides information about the tool. It does not display private channel reports, provide a public analytics service, or collect Google sign-ins.

Why Google access is requested

The desktop tool requests read-only access to the YouTube account to identify the intended channel and list its uploads, and read-only access to YouTube Analytics to retrieve performance reports. It checks that the authorised channel matches the channel configured by the owner.

It does not request permission to upload, delete, edit or publish videos. Google authorisation takes place on Google's own consent screens.

Data stays under the owner's control

The collector saves reports on the owner's computer. OAuth tokens are encrypted for the owner's Windows account. The owner may choose to share selected reports with an AI assistant for interpretation. See the privacy notice for details.
